About PROVO

PROVO is a digital media verification project built to address the growing challenges of generative AI, manipulated media, and misinformation.

Our platform focuses on Content Credentials and the Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA) standard.

Our Mission

To make digital content provenance more transparent, accessible, and understandable for everyone.

Our Core Features

  • C2PA manifest inspection
  • Digital signature verification
  • Signing certificate revocation checks
  • Metadata and timestamp analysis
  • Detailed provenance verification reports

How PROVO Works

Step 1: Upload Media

Select an image or video that you want to investigate.

Step 2: Extract Metadata

PROVO examines the file for available C2PA manifests and provenance information.

Step 3: Verify Digital Signatures

The system checks the cryptographic signatures and available certificate information.

Step 4: Check Revocation and Timestamps

PROVO examines available certificate revocation information and analyzes relevant timestamps for inconsistencies.

Step 5: Generate a Report

Receive a report explaining the verification results, available provenance information, and any detected concerns.

Important: A valid C2PA signature establishes the integrity and attributed provenance of signed information. It does not independently prove that the depicted content is true or that it was not AI-generated.
